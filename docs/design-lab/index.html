Design lab

6 directions · same fixture (Blaine, staged week-11 state) · boards + SVP cover only · live site → · NOTES.md

Variant 1 · baseline
Warm Broadsheet

The chat-sketch direction kept honest: Fraunces + Plex on warm newsprint, solid-vs-washed boards, two accents total.

The gentlest option — cream + serif is also the most predictable of the six.

Variant 2 · locked
Editorial Three-Tier

The classic sports desk: Oswald condensed caps for identity, Source Serif for voice, mono for the game layer, thick section rules.

Most conventional — it looks like a real sports section because it borrows the uniform, so it's the least ownable.

Variant 3 · structural
Manager Dossier

The owner IS the page: oversized 5:7 persona portrait, 84px condensed nameplate, record-card form strip, picks as a squad.

Spends a full screen per owner — brilliant for profiles, expensive for a 12-manager scan-the-league view.

Variant 4 · dense
Overnight Wire

Teletype dispatch: all-mono, heavy rules, the whole panel group (16 picks) on one screen, bracketed [floor → bank → ceiling] ranges, rubber-stamped DEAD.

Maximum information, minimum warmth — brackets have to hide on narrow phones to keep rows on one line.

Variant 5 · club system
Three Mastheads

One kit, three club identities: panel/family/church each get an accent (oxblood/pine/navy) and their own masthead rule; Board 2 rides as a sidebar rail.

Three accents means policing three palettes — the restraint rule gets harder to enforce as pages multiply.

Variant 6 · analytical
The Ledger

Premium-analytical: one unified card where the projection is an italic column inside the exact table — certainty carried by typography (roman vs italic), not by a second board.

Merging the boards is elegant but softens the hard "exact vs projection" wall the two-board architecture was built to defend.

Fixture note: Blaine's real 2025-replay data is fully decided (every pick clinched, floor = ceiling), which renders range bars as dots — so all six variants stage the same invented week-11 state to exercise LIVE/CLINCHED/DEAD, both badge directions, and real floor–ceiling spreads. See NOTES.md.
