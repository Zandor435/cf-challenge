## matt_m — "The Realtor"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Matt Merlene

**backstory:**

Newly minted real estate agent, which means he is currently in the drumming-up-
business phase and everyone in the WhatsApp knows it. Comes to it honestly —
Airbnb operator, commercial brokering, a real track record — but there is no
version of this where the group lets a fresh license go unremarked. Ohio born,
Browns first, Buckeyes second, Cleveland everything after that. Trades movie
and comedy recommendations with Zach at a volume that suggests neither of them
has other hobbies, and their taste is bad in exactly the same way, which is why
it works.

He is the only man in this group whose portrait has no school on it at all.
Everyone else got a program; Matt got a billboard advertising his own phone
number. He has never once complained about this, which is the most damning part
of it.

The board is three unders and one over, which is a man walking you through
comps. Vanderbilt under 5.5 is the sharp one and he knows it: the market took
two good years and priced them as a permanent condition, and permanent
conditions are exactly what he sells against. North Carolina under 4.5 is the
same trade with the volume turned up. Texas Tech under 10.5 is the one that
will hurt — he faded the one program in the country that went out and actually
paid for a roster, which is a thing he would have noticed in any other
business. Penn State over 8.5 is the listing, and he believes in the listing.

- **running_gag:** The hustle. Every conversation eventually becomes a listing.
- **draft_tendency:** Sells you on his picks. Hard. Three of the four are unders, so what he is
  mostly selling is nothing happening.
- **fatal_flaw:** Cannot tell the difference between a thing being cheap and a thing being bad —
  a fine instinct in real estate, an expensive one here.

## hauck — "The Host"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Matt Hauck

**backstory:**

Hosts the game, which grants him diplomatic immunity roughly none of the time.
Works in alcohol, tastes wine seriously, lifts, and walks his lab nearly every
day — a genuinely well-rounded life that he then spends every autumn feeding
into the Cleveland Browns. Ohio born and Ohio State made, which at this table
is the rare credential: most of this group roots for things they picked up
somewhere along the way, and Hauck actually comes from the place he roots for.
He has the receipts, he knows he has the receipts, and he will produce them
unprompted. He married a veterinarian, which given the dog situation reads less
like romance and more like vertical integration.

Which makes the portrait a problem, because they put him in Kentucky blue. He
took Kentucky over 4.5 and has been explaining it ever since: a four-and-a-half
win floor in the SEC is not a projection, it is an insult, and somebody at this
table was going to take the other side of an insult. Nobody asked him to become
a Wildcat about it. He has leaned in anyway, the way he leans into everything.

Ohio State over 9.5 is the honest one and the only pick he does not have to
argue for. Navy over 7.5 is the tasting-flight pour — he likes an offense
nobody gets a real practice week against. Miami under 10.5 is the bottle he
sent back: a ceiling that tall resting that heavily on staying healthy is not a
bet, it is a hope, and he does not drink hope.

- **running_gag:** The receipts. He has them, he knows he has them, and he will produce them.
- **draft_tendency:** Curated. Treats a draft board like a tasting flight.
- **fatal_flaw:** Confuses a number being disrespectful with a number being wrong.

## bryan — "Private Equity"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Bryan

**backstory:**

The youngest at the table and the best player at it, which is an annoying
combination to sit across from. Runs his own PE group, engaged to Samantha,
neighbor to Zach, owner of a brown lab named Tater, and — the detail that
undoes all of the above — an enthusiastic decorator of his home with ferns. The
man has a green thumb. He is also a deeply serious Ohio State and Browns fan
and takes an enormous amount of incoming for it. Takes it well. Then takes
Zach’s money.

They rendered him in Virginia colors. Not one person, Bryan included, has
produced an explanation. He does not have a Virginia opinion. He has never had
a Virginia opinion. He is now, permanently, a Cavalier.

The board is a leveraged buyout. He shorted Michigan at 8.5 and Texas at 9.5 —
the two most-hyped brands in the sport — and rolled the proceeds into Kent
State over 3.5 and South Florida over 8.5. Two names sold at the top, two small
caps bought at the bottom. The Michigan under is the one place where the model
and the Ohio State fan in him agreed completely, and he has never looked more
at peace. South Florida over 8.5 is the one his day job should have talked him
out of: that is buying a mid-major after the good year, at the number the good
year created. It is also the only head-to-head on this board — Todd has the
under — so two men who have never once cared about South Florida will now watch
every snap of it for exactly opposite reasons.

- **running_gag:** The ferns. Also: he wins.
- **draft_tendency:** Ruthless. Reads the board and doesn't blink.
- **fatal_flaw:** Will always take the clever side of a trade, including the times the obvious
  side was the correct one.
- **rival:** `zach`

## todd — "Atlas"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Todd

**backstory:**

The most relaxed man in the group by a wide margin. Works support at Autodesk,
drives a Rivian, and owns an enormous black lab mix named Atlas who is, by
consensus, the largest dog anyone here has met. Die-hard Browns, which for a
laid-back guy means he has developed a serenity the rest of the group has not —
decades of practice at accepting outcomes. Jokes constantly, never escalates.

He is the only manager with two portraits and both of them put him in Ohio
State red. He then built a board with not one Big Ten team on it. Wears the
shirt, will not back the conference, and sees no tension in this whatsoever.

What he does back is a ceiling. Boise State over 7.5 and Iowa State over 4.5
are the same pick made twice: programs that have quietly cleared those numbers
again and again while the market kept filing it under surprise. The Iowa State
over is close to free and he took it in about four seconds. Then he turned
around and put unders on South Florida and James Madison, which is where the
thesis comes apart — James Madison is a group-of-five program that reloads, and
fading it is the exact bet he spent the other half of his board arguing
against. The South Florida under is this board’s only head-to-head, with Bryan
on the other side. The most relaxed man in the group has been handed the one
pick he cannot be relaxed about. He intends to be relaxed about it anyway.

- **running_gag:** The dog. The Rivian. The unbotherable calm.
- **draft_tendency:** Doesn't overthink. Picks and moves on.
- **fatal_flaw:** Holds two opposite theories about group-of-five football at the same time and
  has never once been troubled by it.

## zach — "The Commissioner"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Zach

**backstory:**

Wake Forest, LSU, and the only man at this table who does not care about the
Cleveland Browns, which is its own kind of standing. Built the entire scoring
engine, projection model and website for a pool that could have run on a
napkin. Contributes reliably at poker in the sense that his money reliably ends
up elsewhere, mostly Bryan’s. Has decided that the correct response to being
bad at one game is to build a second game and administer it.

His portrait is the only one that is not a sideline. It is a press conference,
behind a Wake Forest podium, under a nameplate reading FAT ZACH — HEAD COACH.
He is the one man here whose art is his actual school and the one man here who
did not draft it. Wake Forest opened at a number he has called defensible,
which is the word he uses when the model disagrees with him and he has decided
to let it win.

Four picks, four overs, from the person who wrote the projection model — which
makes the commissioner the single most optimistic man in a league he built
himself. He says the numbers told him to. He wrote the numbers. LSU over 8.5 is
at least honest; that one is just his school. Oregon over 10.5 is the one that
should worry him, because asking a team for eleven wins is not optimism, it is
a toll you pay in November.

- **running_gag:** Loses at poker, writes the scoring for everything else.
- **draft_tendency:** Overthinks it, then takes the model's number anyway.
- **fatal_flaw:** Built the model, trusts the model, and drafted the four teams the model liked
  best — which is either rigor or a closed loop, and he cannot tell you which.
- **rival:** `bryan`

## josh — "The Sommelier"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Josh

**backstory:**

Works at a vineyard in Charlottesville, which makes the Stanford pick — Palo
Alto, wine country, 3.5-win football program — either a lifestyle brand or
the most romantic over on the board. Single, actively dating in
Charlottesville via the apps, and the owner of an omnidirectional VR
treadmill, which is a piece of equipment that costs more than most people's
first car and exists so he can walk through video games instead of sitting
through them. He has complained, more than once, about his back hurting from
bending down in too many games. On the treadmill. The one he bought to be
more active. Florida under, BYU under, Virginia over — three
mid-major-energy calls that read like a man who watches enough football to
have opinions but not enough to second-guess them. The Stanford pick is the
tell: nobody takes Stanford over 3.5 without either knowing something or
believing something, and Josh is definitely one of those.

- **running_gag:** The VR treadmill. He bought it to move more. His back hurts from moving on
  it. Nobody has resolved this for him.
- **draft_tendency:** Vibes-based. Will pick a team because something about them feels right, and
  will not be able to explain what that something is.
- **fatal_flaw:** Will absolutely check his picks between Tinder swipes and call that
  research.

## mark — "Dave's Bread"

- **tone:** roast
- **display_name:** Mark

**backstory:**

Indiana University, class of 2010, which makes the coach portrait in the
National Champions jersey either prophecy or the most optimistic thing on
this website. Sells bread for a living — Flowers Foods, the Dave's Killer
Bread people — out of Charlottesville with two small kids at home. Indiana
over 10.5 is not a reach — it's the one pick on this board where the man
actually went to the school. Notre Dame under 11.5 is the counterweight: if
you believe in your program, you have to believe the other guy's program is
overvalued. Maryland under and Duke over round it out — ACC mid-tier, read
correctly, no sentiment involved. The most honest board in the group.

- **running_gag:** The one man whose coach portrait is actually his school. Nobody else in this
  group can say that.
- **draft_tendency:** Homer where it counts, analyst everywhere else. The IU pick is blood; the
  other three are spreadsheet.
- **fatal_flaw:** Two small children and a bread route. The research window is 9pm to whenever
  he falls asleep on the couch.
